## Corner 1.1.2

Downloads: **Corner-Windows.zip** (Windows 10 and 11) and **Corner-Mac-Swift.zip** (Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel,
macOS 12 or newer). The [README](https://github.com/isasardar1-coder/corner#readme) says how to open them the first time.

This release is for the Mac. On Windows nothing changes but the version number.

### How to update

- **Mac:** download `Corner-Mac-Swift.zip` and replace Corner.app in Applications. Your tasks are kept.
  The first open needs the same step as before (see the README).
- **Windows, from 1.1.0 or 1.1.1:** nothing to do; Corner fetches it by itself.
- **Windows, from 1.0.0:** download `Corner-Windows.zip`, quit Corner (right-click its tray icon), and put the new
  `Corner.exe` in place of the old one. Keep your other files. From then on it updates itself.

### New on the Mac: it now looks like the Windows app

- **The corner panel opens on one line:** the next task, its circle and its timer. Move the pointer onto it and a
  small header (progress ring, Full Mode, Close) and the row of chips come in: "All tasks" opens the list, + the add
  box, and the wave the energy chart. Settings are opened from Full Mode.
- **Full Mode has the new layout.** Tasks side: the list, the add box and the plan. Energy side: the next task, the
  chart, three round icons for Slept, Debt and Need (click one to open it), "Log a nap" and "Rate now", Routines and
  Done. An applied plan stands as an upright "Today's plan" strip beside its tasks. Settings > Panel > "Full Mode
  sides" swaps the two.
- **Glass is clear glass now**, as on Windows: a thin veil over what is behind the panel, a bright rim, glossy pills,
  and no colour of its own except the red of "overdue". It turns dark when macOS is in Dark Mode.

### What to know

- These were built and checked on GitHub's Mac as pictures, beside pictures of the Windows app. Nobody has yet
  clicked through them on a Mac's own screen, and the blur behind Glass cannot be pictured there at all. If something
  sits in the wrong place, flickers or is hard to read, please say so.
- In Full Mode a long list scrolls inside its card.
