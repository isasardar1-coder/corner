## Corner 1.1.4

Downloads: **Corner-Windows.zip** (Windows 10 and 11) and **Corner-Mac-Swift.zip** (Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel,
macOS 12 or newer). The [README](https://github.com/isasardar1-coder/corner#readme) says how to open them the first time.

### How to update

- **Windows, from 1.1.0 or newer, and Mac, from 1.1.3:** nothing to do; Corner fetches it by itself.
- **Mac, before 1.1.3:** download `Corner-Mac-Swift.zip` once more and replace Corner.app in Applications. Your tasks
  are kept. The first open needs the same step as before (see the README).
- **Windows, from 1.0.0:** download `Corner-Windows.zip`, quit Corner (right-click its tray icon), and put the new
  `Corner.exe` in place of the old one. Keep your other files.

### New on Windows and on the Mac

- **Full Mode's sections can be moved.** Point at a section and drag it by the dots at its right edge, to another place
  or into the other column. Esc cancels. Settings > Panel puts them all back.
- **Full Mode is calmer:** the section with the current task is gone (the corner panel keeps it). To put a started timer
  back to its full length, right-click it in the list.
- **"Log a nap" and "Rate now" are icons** between Slept, Debt and Need.
- **A line-drawn logo** in the header, in the colour of the skin. Pointing at it shows the date and how many tasks are open.

### New on the Mac

- **Click sounds**, as on Windows, with a switch in Settings > Panel.
- **Glass is light or dark by the wallpaper** behind the panel. Over other windows, or a wallpaper it cannot read, it
  follows Dark Mode.
- **Glass is the first skin**, as on Windows. A Mac on which no skin was ever chosen changes from Sage to Glass.

### What to know

- The Mac version was built and drawn on GitHub's Mac, but moving sections has not yet been tried there with a real
  mouse. If something does not move as it should, please say what happened.
