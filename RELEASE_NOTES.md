## Corner 1.1.5

Downloads: **Corner-Windows.zip** (Windows 10 and 11) and **Corner-Mac-Swift.zip** (Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel,
macOS 12 or newer). The [README](https://github.com/isasardar1-coder/corner#readme) says how to open them the first time.

### How to update

- **Windows, from 1.1.0 or newer, and Mac, from 1.1.3:** nothing to do; Corner fetches it by itself.
- **Mac, before 1.1.3:** download `Corner-Mac-Swift.zip` once more and replace Corner.app in Applications. Your tasks
  are kept. The first open needs the same step as before (see the README).
- **Windows, from 1.0.0:** download `Corner-Windows.zip`, quit Corner (right-click its tray icon), and put the new
  `Corner.exe` in place of the old one. Keep your other files.

### New on Windows and on the Mac

- **The task you are working on no longer looks done.** Its circle used to carry a tick while its timer ran (or, when
  planning by priority, once it had been started). The circle is now empty like the others; pointing at any circle
  shows a faint tick, and clicking it still ticks the task off.
- **Full Mode is only as tall as it needs to be.** With an empty day, or the routines closed, the panel is shorter
  instead of keeping a blank space at the bottom.
