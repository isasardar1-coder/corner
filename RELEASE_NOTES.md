## Corner 1.1.6

Downloads: **Corner-Windows.zip** (Windows 10 and 11) and **Corner-Mac-Swift.zip** (Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel,
macOS 12 or newer). The [README](https://github.com/isasardar1-coder/corner#readme) says how to open them the first time.

### How to update

- **Windows, from 1.1.0 or newer, and Mac, from 1.1.3:** nothing to do; Corner fetches it by itself.
- **Mac, before 1.1.3:** download `Corner-Mac-Swift.zip` once more and replace Corner.app in Applications. Your tasks
  are kept. The first open needs the same step as before (see the README).
- **Windows, from 1.0.0:** download `Corner-Windows.zip`, quit Corner (right-click its tray icon), and put the new
  `Corner.exe` in place of the old one. Keep your other files.

### New on Windows and on the Mac

- **Done shows today.** The Done list now holds what you finished today, not everything since you started using
  Corner. The progress ring counts today too. Nothing is deleted: older finished tasks are only hidden, and "Clear"
  still removes them.
- **How long, and from when to when.** Each finished task says how long you worked on it and between which times,
  for example "1h 26m · 12:12pm–1:38pm". The length is your timed stretches added up, so pauses are not counted.
  A task ticked off without a timer shows the time it was ticked.
- **Earlier days, in Full Mode.** With the Done card open, the arrows in its head ("‹ Today ›") step back through
  earlier days and forward again. Each day lists what you finished, with the same times, and the head shows the
  day's total time worked. Days with nothing logged are skipped.
