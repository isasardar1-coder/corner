CORNER
A small to-do list that lives in a corner of your screen and plans your day
around your energy: hard tasks on your peaks, easy ones in your afternoon dip.

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Nothing to install.


STARTING IT

1. Move this whole folder somewhere that is yours, for example Documents.
   (Not Program Files: Corner keeps its notes beside Corner.exe.)
2. Double-click Corner.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app is not from
   the Microsoft Store. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. A welcome window asks your name, how much sleep you need and what you do
   most days. That's it.

Corner then lives in the top-right corner of your screen: move your pointer
there to see your tasks. Its icon sits in the system tray (near the clock);
right-click it for more, including Quit.

Corner starts with Windows. To stop that: Settings (the gear) > Start with
Windows, or right-click the tray icon.


EACH DAY

- In the morning it asks how you slept, and draws your energy for the day.
- Add tasks with an effort level and a length, e.g.  Write report !high 45m
  (!low, !med, !high; 20m, 1h ...).
- Routines (in the energy view): your daily things. Press "Add to today" to
  send them to your tasks.
- "Plan my day" fits your tasks to your energy, from now until bedtime.
- Settings > Planning > "Plan by": Schedule gives each task a time. Priority
  gives only an order: press play on a task to start a stopwatch, pause when
  you stop. "Plan my day" then says what comes after what, not when.
- Corner notes what you work on and when (work.csv). After a few days it
  offers what you usually do at this time of day under the add box.
- Click the energy % to type how you really feel; the chart can follow.
- A card with a ring appears 10 minutes before each energy peak and
  20 minutes before the dip.


YOUR DATA

Everything stays on your PC, in this folder:
  tasks.json, routines.json, sleep.json, settings.json   what Corner needs
  done.csv    every task you finish (opens in Excel)
  days.csv    one line per day: done, open, by effort, sleep
  work.csv    every stretch of work: start, length, energy at start and end
Settings > Your record shows the last 7 days.

To change the sound, replace ping.wav with any .wav file of your own.


UPDATES

Corner updates itself. It looks for a new version on GitHub a minute after it
starts and then every 6 hours, downloads it, and restarts once you're not
using it. Your tasks and notes stay as they are. Settings > Updates turns this
off, and "Check now" looks straight away.


REMOVING IT

Right-click the tray icon > Quit, turn off "Start with Windows" first if you
like, then delete the folder.
